My name is Meris Bihorac, and I am living in Austria, working as an Oracle Database Administrator at [DBConcepts GmbH](https://www.dbconcepts.com/en/).

My fascination with technology began early-around the age of 15 or 16 when I discovered the world of bug bounty hunting. That was my first real exposure to operating systems, databases, and security. From there, the journey truly began. I started diving into vulnerabilities, exploring security flaws, and uncovering issues like SQL injections, XSS vulnerabilities, and much more.

After high school, I continued my path by studying at HTL Donaustadt in Austria, where I deepened my technical knowledge and worked on several projects-primarily involving databases. While I enjoyed development, my bug hunting background always pushed me toward problem-solving and troubleshooting complex systems.

That passion naturally led me to pursue a career as a Database Administrator (DBA). At DBConcepts GmbH, I get to work with a wide range of customers and diverse system configurations. No two days are the same, each one brings new challenges and opportunities to learn something new.
