My name is Meris Bihorac, and I live in Austria, where I work as an Oracle Database Administrator at [DBConcepts GmbH](https://www.dbconcepts.com/en/).

My fascination with technology began early, around the age of 15 or 16, when I discovered the world of bug bounty hunting. That was my first real exposure to operating systems, databases, and security. From there, the journey truly began. I started exploring vulnerabilities, security flaws, and issues such as SQL injection, cross-site scripting (XSS), and much more.

After high school, I continued my education at HTL Donaustadt in Austria, where I deepened my technical knowledge and worked on several projects, primarily involving databases. While I enjoyed development, my background in bug hunting continually drew me toward problem-solving, troubleshooting, and understanding complex systems.

That passion naturally led me to pursue a career as a Database Administrator (DBA). At DBConcepts GmbH, I work with a wide range of customers and diverse system configurations. No two days are the same; each brings new challenges and opportunities to learn something new.
